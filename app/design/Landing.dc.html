Three steps between you and your next certification.

Courses stay on the official platforms. TekCert guides you, tracks your progress and makes your achievements visible.

I.Tell us your specialtyIn ING-4 and ING-5 you follow your specialty's path. From ING-1 to ING-3, pick your target specialty or start with the Fundamentals.
II.Go chapter by chapterTick off the official syllabus as you revise, follow the recommended resources and practise with practice tests.
III.Pass the exam, get it verifiedRequest a seat at a session, then upload your proof. Once verified, the certification joins your collection.
